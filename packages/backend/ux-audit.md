## Observation on what feels slow, confusion and risky

Screen is tiny — only shows a few lines at a time
No internet — USSD works but they might not understand the flow
Literacy issues —  some may struggle to type names
No back button awareness — they might not know 0 goes back
Timeout — 180 seconds might not be enough for slow typers 
Phone number format — requiring 0 + 9 digits might confuse people
Language — even Kiswahili might not be their first language and english

## The improvents I would make 
-Add voice support for the illiterate people 
-Shorten menu text to reduce reading
-Add a confirmation step that reads back what they entered
